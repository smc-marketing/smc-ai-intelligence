Two data stacks, on purpose. Moz Pro runs one response per prompt on four engines and owns the week-over-week read at the top of this page. DataForSEO runs three samples per prompt on ChatGPT and Gemini, which is what makes the prompt-level rates further down defensible — a single draw cannot tell movement from variance. Where they disagree on absolute level, the prompt sets differ; where they agree on direction, that is the real signal.

Both are accurate; they answer different questions.

Priorities this week

Fix — the Full Tuition Scholarship page
Answer the question the prompt asks: who qualifies, any income threshold, and one sentence that reads "bachelor's degree, no tuition." The models need a liftable claim. On that prompt USC Upstate is named in all 6 responses and SCC in 5 of 6; the page is cited and SMC is never named.
Fix — the transfer-credit page opening
It is cited 28 times a week and named in zero open answers. Lead with "affordable online bachelor's degree, transfer up to X credits" in the first paragraph rather than describing policy. Same content, answer-shaped.
Keep — branded and comparison content
48 of 48 responses with citation attached on both engines. Whatever the site is doing on branded and head-to-head prompts is working; the work this cycle belongs entirely to the open tier.
Fix — duplicate and outdated catalogs
One canonical 2024–25 catalog URL, 301 the other three. Superseded notice on both 2022–23 PDFs. 10 citations a week still resolve to the 2022–23 edition and 18 to out-of-date catalogs overall, on a site whose 2024–25 PDF is its single best-cited document.
Ask — what Moz's tracked prompt set actually is
Moz produces one number per engine that none of our other tools produce, so it is earning its place. But we cannot audit its prompt list from the dashboard, and its AI data arrives by screenshot while its own API covers links, keywords and Brand Authority only. Worth asking whether Moz exposes the tracked prompts anywhere exportable before renewal, because that is the only thing standing between us and automating the whole section.
