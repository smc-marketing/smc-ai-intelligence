SMC is named on 44 of 74 questions in at least one engine, but the engines disagree: Gemini 59.5%, ChatGPT 50.0%, Google AI Mode 43.2%. 30 questions are missed by every engine, but only 14 of those are ours to win — the other 16 name a competitor directly, so SMC appearing there would be the surprise. The 14 are the working list: no amount of engine-specific tuning fixes them. Worth noting: every question any engine names SMC on is also named by Gemini — Gemini's 44 questions are the full set, so it is the most generous of the three.

But being named and being linked are different things. ChatGPT links to smcsc.edu 213 times; Gemini links to it 6 times. (Google AI Mode sits at 201.) A mention without a link sends you no traffic.
