Sep 22 showed Direct sessions drop 93% and Chrome sessions drop 97%. Check GTM for tag changes, consent mode updates, or configuration errors on that date before using any traffic data in leadership reports.
🔴 Priority — Page speed (49)
Unchanged for two weeks with 7 pending tasks. Page speed affects both traditional SEO rankings and AI crawlability. Friday SEO task list priority — work through the Visby pending tasks list.
🟡 Investigate — ChatGPT citation surge
Citations jumped from 11 to 97 in one week. Find out what changed — which pages are now being cited, and why. Replicate whatever drove this to sustain the momentum.
🟡 Monitor — Perplexity declining
-2.3% mention rate week over week. Perplexity is citation-driven (690 citations) — check if any high-DA pages linking to smcsc.edu changed or were removed recently.
🟡 Content — Facilities narrative
ChatGPT flags "facilities criticism" at score 25 (negative). Adding recent campus improvement content, student testimonials about facilities, and photo-rich pages could shift this theme positive.
