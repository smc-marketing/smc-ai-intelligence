Strategic priorities — board takeaways

Win to defend
SMC is mentioned on every engine whenever a prompt names it, and leads the tracked set on all four. It is strongest on cost, how-to-apply, reviews and ranking prompts (75% to 100% in Visby).
Gap to close — open prompts
15 of 35 open prompts return no SMC mention on any engine, and "best two year colleges in south carolina" is 0% on ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Answer-first pages and FAQ markup on admission requirements, financial aid, tuition and online-class topics are the most direct fix.
Gap to close — online programs
Sentiment on online degrees ranges from 92 (ChatGPT) to 25 (Gemini, Perplexity). Consistent program names, pricing and start-date information across smcsc.edu and online.smcsc.edu would give the engines one story to repeat.
Brand name clarity
Another college shares the "SMC" abbreviation. Using "Spartanburg Methodist College" or "SMC Spartanburg" in titles, schema and tracked prompts reduces the mix-up.
Quick win — page speed
Page Speed is 49 with 7 tasks pending, unchanged from last week. It affects both traditional SEO and how reliably AI crawlers reach the site.
