Why this differs from Moz
Moz tracks 74 prompts about Spartanburg colleges. SE Ranking counts every keyword in its database, including thousands of program, nursing, technical and workforce searches where SCC has pages and SMC does not offer the program. SCC's lead here reflects catalog size as much as AI performance.
ChatGPT is close to even
SMC is named in 15 ChatGPT answers to SCC's 17 and USC Upstate's 12, so SMC holds 88% of SCC's brand presence there, compared with 5% on AI Overviews and 3% on AI Mode.
Google AI Overviews is the volume gap
AI Overviews link to sccsc.edu 3,098 times and smcsc.edu 72 times. The lever is program-level pages that target the keywords SMC does serve: online bachelor's, transfer, BAMS, business and criminal justice.

Priorities this week

Defend — Google AI Mode
Best placement, best sentiment and rising citations. Keep the pages Google is citing current, especially admissions, cost and financial aid, since those are what it uses to frame SMC.
Close — online program content
Publish one list-format page of every SMC Online program with format, length and cost. This targets ChatGPT's citation gap and Gemini's "online learning challenges" theme, and answers several of the 15 open prompts SMC misses.
Fix — "SMC" ambiguity
Add "Spartanburg Methodist College" as the name and "SMC" as alternateName in Organization schema, and lead with the full name in page titles and llms.txt.
Fix — retire old catalogs
Add a "superseded" notice linking to the current catalog on the 2021–22 and 2022–23 catalog PDFs, or noindex them, so ChatGPT stops citing outdated program and cost details.
Clean up — tracked prompts
Retire the 16 SCC- and Wofford-named Moz prompts (two misspell Wofford), add USC Upstate comparisons, and fix the Visby "smc campus life" prompt. Expect a one-time jump in the headline rate when this happens.
