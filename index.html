Opportunities & how to act on them

📄 Biggest citation gap
The top cited page in Profound's data is uopeople.edu/blog/10-most-popular-online-colleges-in-south-carolina/ with 23 citations — SMC is not mentioned on it. This single page is driving more AI citations than your entire work-study page.
How to fix
Reach out to University of the People to request SMC's inclusion, or publish a competing page — "Best Online Colleges in South Carolina" — optimized to outrank it. The second option keeps the traffic on smcsc.edu permanently.
🔗 Citation coverage gap
smcsc.edu is cited in 26.4% of tracked prompts vs USC Upstate at 36.3%. The gap is driven by Institution-type citations (directories, rankings) which make up 61.45% of all AI citations but only 7.84% are from smcsc.edu's own domain.
How to fix
Complete and enhance profiles on niche.com, collegeraptor.com, collegesimply.com, and communitycollegereview.com — all of which appear in citation data already. Submitting program data to research.com rankings would also add institutional citations quickly.
📝 Program clarity — Perplexity
Perplexity is flagging "program clarity issues" — Moz shows AI is confusing SMC with other similarly-named programs. This is a schema and structure problem more than a content problem.
How to fix
Add FAQ schema markup to program pages with clear Q&A pairs: "What programs does Spartanburg Methodist College offer?" Add the institution's full name and location to every page's meta description and structured data.
🎓 Online major availability
ChatGPT scores online major availability at 25 (negative). This is likely because SMC's online program offerings aren't clearly mapped in AI-citable format — the programs exist but AI can't easily enumerate them.
How to fix
Create or update a single comprehensive "SMC Online Degree Programs" landing page that lists every online program with brief descriptions, credit hours, and cost. This becomes a highly citable reference page for all engines.
💰 Work-study page performing well
smcsc.edu/admissions/financial-aid/work-study/ is your #1 cited page at 14 citations — more than any other smcsc.edu page. It's clearly structured in a way AI engines trust and cite.
How to replicate
Use this page as a template. Look at its structure — clear headers, specific facts, Q&A format — and apply the same approach to your financial aid overview, scholarship page, and each major program page.
📈 ChatGPT financial aid content
Financial aid opportunities scores 95 positive on ChatGPT — the highest theme score in the report. SMC's financial aid and full tuition scholarship content is genuinely excellent at getting cited.
How to build on it
Create a "SMC vs [competitor] cost comparison" page that cites actual net price data. This type of content scores extremely well in AI citation because it answers a high-intent comparison question with specific, citable facts.
